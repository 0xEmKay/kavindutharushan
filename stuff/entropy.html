    the maths

    what actually helps

    • Length beats cleverness. Every extra character multiplies the work for an attacker.
    • Random beats memorable. Substituting a for @ is in every cracking dictionary.
    • A passphrase of 5 random words from a 7,776-word list is about 64.6 bits, and 6 words is about 77.5 bits.
    • Use a different password everywhere, kept in a password manager. A strong reused password is still one breach away from useless.
