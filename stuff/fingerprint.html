what you can do

  • Firefox's Strict tracking protection blocks known fingerprinting scripts. The privacy.resistFingerprinting setting in about:config makes many values generic, but it can break some sites and forces the timezone to UTC.
  • Brave adds small random noise to canvas and WebGL results ("farbling") by default, so they change between sessions.
  • Tor Browser tries to make every user look the same, which is the strongest approach.
  • uBlock Origin blocks many tracking scripts, but it can't change what your browser reveals to a page you choose to visit.
  • Standing out is the problem. Unusual settings and extra fonts make you more unique, so changing many settings by hand often backfires.
